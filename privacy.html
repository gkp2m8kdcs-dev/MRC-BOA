Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how MRC BOA (the “App”, “we”, “us”) handles information when you use our personal finance and investment tracker. By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

Summary: MRC BOA stores your financial data exclusively on your device. We do not collect, transmit, sell, share, or analyze any personal information. There are no accounts, no servers, and no third-party trackers.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

MRC BOA does not collect, transmit, or share any personal, financial, or device information. Specifically, we do not:

  • Require you to create an account or sign in.
  • Collect your name, email, phone number, address, or any contact details.
  • Collect account numbers, balances, transactions, holdings, or any financial data you enter.
  • Use analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs, or third-party tracking tools.
  • Share data with brokers, banks, exchanges, data brokers, marketing partners, or advertisers.

2. Where Your Data Lives

All financial data you enter — including accounts, transactions, categories, budgets, goals, investment holdings, and attached receipt photos — is stored locally on your iPhone using Apple’s standard secure storage. This data is protected by your device’s built-in encryption and access controls (Face ID, Touch ID, or passcode).

The data does not leave your device under normal operation of the App.

3. Receipt and Statement Photos

If you attach a photo to a transaction (via the camera or photo library), that image is stored locally within the App’s sandbox and is not uploaded to any server. The App uses your camera and photo library only for the explicit purpose of attaching images to a transaction, as described in the system permission prompts.

4. Permissions Requested

MRC BOA requests only the permissions it needs to provide its features:

  • Camera: to capture photos of receipts and statements that you choose to attach to a transaction.
  • Photo Library (Read): to allow you to attach an existing image to a transaction.
  • Photo Library (Add): to save exported portfolio or report images you explicitly choose to share.

You can deny any of these permissions and still use the rest of the App.

5. Data Export and Reset

You may export your data to CSV at any time via the App’s Settings menu. You may also reset the App to its sample data with a single tap. We never receive a copy of exported data.

6. Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. Because the App does not collect information at all, this requirement is satisfied by design.

7. International Use

Because no data leaves your device, the location or jurisdiction from which you use the App does not affect your data’s privacy. Your data remains on your device wherever you are.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by a new “Last updated” date above. Because the App does not collect data, any such update is unlikely to reduce your existing privacy protections.

9. Disclaimer

MRC BOA is a tracking and journaling tool only. It does not provide investment, financial, or tax advice. All investment prices and balances are entered manually by you. Past performance does not guarantee future returns.

10. Contact
