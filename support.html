Frequently Asked Questions

Where is my financial data stored?
All data you enter into MRC BOA is stored locally on your iPhone. The App does not send your data to any server. See the Privacy Policy for full details.
Do I need to create an account?
No. MRC BOA has no accounts, no logins, and no signups. Open the App and start tracking — that’s it.
How do I record a transaction?
From the Transactions tab, tap the “+” button, choose the account, type (income, expense, or transfer), category, amount, date, and an optional note. You can also attach a photo of the receipt.
How does the investment portfolio feature work?
Add a holding with the symbol/name, type (stock, ETF, crypto, etc.), quantity, average cost, current price, and purchase date. MRC BOA calculates market value, unrealized gain/loss, and allocation. Price charts use a 90-day visual indicator. All values are entered manually — the App does not connect to any exchange.
Can I export my data?
Yes. Go to Settings → Export and choose CSV. The file will be saved to your Files app, ready to share or back up. You can also reset the App to its sample data at any time.
How do I back up my data if I get a new phone?
We recommend exporting your data to CSV regularly. Standard iOS device backups (iCloud or iTunes) will include the App’s local storage. Note that the App does not sync data to the cloud automatically.
Why are some charts interactive?
MRC BOA uses Apple’s native Charts framework, which supports touch interaction. You can drag across any chart to inspect individual data points with precise values and dates.
Is my data encrypted?
Your data is protected by your device’s built-in encryption — the same encryption that protects the rest of your iPhone. Enable a passcode (and ideally Face ID / Touch ID) for the strongest protection.
Does MRC BOA provide investment advice?
No. MRC BOA is a tracking and journaling tool only. It does not provide investment, financial, or tax advice. All prices and balances are entered manually. Past performance does not guarantee future returns.
